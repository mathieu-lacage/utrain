The TUI has a top row of workspaces -- Runs, Sweeps, Compare and System --
in place of its header. `Alt` with a title's underlined letter goes to that
workspace -- `Alt+R` Runs, `Alt+S` Sweeps, `Alt+C` Compare, `Alt+Y` System --
as do `F1`-`F4` where the terminal passes function keys on; pressing the key of
the one you are in, `F10`, or clicking a title drops that
workspace's menu over it: every command, with its key beside it, greyed when it
does not apply to what is selected. The System workspace shows the compute
devices with what runs on each, the images, and the data store, and can add and
delete images and clean the store. `?` now lists every menu.
