The TUI has a top row of workspaces -- Runs, Sweeps, Compare and System --
in place of its header. `Alt` with a title's underlined letter goes to that
workspace -- `Alt+R` Runs, `Alt+S` Sweeps, `Alt+C` Compare, `Alt+Y` System --
as do `F1`-`F4` where the terminal passes function keys on, and a click on its
title. `Alt+M` (or `F10`, or a click on the title you are on) drops the menu of
the workspace you are in over it: every command it has, with its key beside it,
greyed when it does not apply to what is selected. The footer lists only the
keys that move around the workspace, then the menu, goto and help; the
commands are in the menu, and their keys work as before. The System workspace
shows the compute devices with what runs on each, the images, and the data
store, and can add and delete images and clean the store. `?` now lists every
menu.
