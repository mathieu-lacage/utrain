`:` in the TUI opens a goto line at the bottom: a run's name or id prefix goes to
it in Runs (`run/phase` to one of its phases, opening what it is in), `@name` to
a sweep, a workspace's name to that workspace, and `menu` opens the menu of the
workspace you are in (`menu sweeps` goes to Sweeps and opens its) -- everything
the workspace and menu keys reach, for a terminal that does not send them. Names
complete as they are typed; `right` accepts.
