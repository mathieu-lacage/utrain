The Runs workspace is a tree: sweeps, their runs, and each run's phases, opened
and closed with `enter` and the arrows. The right side follows the cursor -- a
sweep's status grid, a queued or configuring run's config, or a run's plots
over its log -- in place of the tabs. `z` zooms one pane, `1`-`3` focus a pane,
`e` edits a configuring run's config in place and shows any other's read-only
(marking what its sweep varied), and `space` marks a run for comparison.
