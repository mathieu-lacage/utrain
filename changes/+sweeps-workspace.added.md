The TUI has a Sweeps workspace (`F2`): the sweeps, and for the one under the
cursor its runs as a grid over its first two axes, what each of its computes is
running and runs next, and its spec. `s`, `p`, `+`, `R`, `S` and `d` start,
pause, extend, retry, cancel and delete it; `enter` on a cell opens that run in
Runs and `space` marks it. `N` makes a sweep -- from the Sweeps workspace by
picking an image and a base config, or from Runs (and from a run's config) around
the selected run -- in a form of the image's config fields, each with what to
sweep it over and the grid's size worked out as you type.
